# Climb Controller

Turn a climbing video into a "someone is controlling the climber" game clip.
Play your clip behind an on-screen controller, press buttons along with it, and screen record.

- **Original version:** https://lai2570.github.io/climb-controller/
- **GTA 5 parody version:** https://lai2570.github.io/climb-controller/gta/

## How to use

1. Film the climb with your phone's normal camera.
2. Open the page on your phone and tap **USE A VIDEO**, then pick the clip.
3. Start your phone's screen recording.
4. Tap **3-2-1 PLAY** and press buttons along with the clip. START = mission passed, hold B = wasted.
5. Stop recording when the clip ends.

**SPEED 0.5x** plays the clip at half speed so the timing is easier. Speed the recording up 2x afterwards.
